Brand paid-search clicks fell 27% in one week. The proposed fix was more budget.

Brand search clicks fell 27% at 96% impression share. More budget could not have bought them back.

Impression share was already 96%. Click elasticity to bid: +0.08, statistically zero. Even at 100% share on every campaign, clicks would still trail last year. Over 80% of the drop was one campaign someone switched off. The money went to channels with headroom.

Store sales went into the Google Shopping bid goal in January 2026, and ecommerce orders per Shopping click fell by half.

Bidding Google Shopping ads on store sales cost a national retailer 5% of its ecommerce orders and added no store orders.

The cost was 5.2% of ecommerce orders site-wide, with a 95% interval of 2.9% to 8.0%. No other channel recovered the lost orders. Store orders per Shopping click fell 13%, and omnichannel sales per click fell 12.7% measured like for like.

How I work

The working rules behind the AI-native claim, with the numbers that can be checked.

Python computes every number. The model only writes the summary.

The anomaly-detection pipeline, built and backtested in September, scores each day against the median of the last eight same weekdays, holiday weeks excluded, and flags at three robust standard deviations plus a minimum practical change. The summary step receives only numbers already computed and writes nothing if it cannot.

Refreshes wait for complete source data.

41 scheduled jobs refresh the dashboards and pipelines. The ones that read Adobe CJA, Google Ads, or Datadog check that yesterday's data has landed first. If it has not, the job exits, deploys nothing, and retries later.

Each dashboard documents its scope and sources.

Each README records scope, grain, upstream tables, refresh schedule, and the request it answered. 85 of them were audited against their live pages on one day in October.

Investigations become reusable playbooks.

A cart-to-checkout investigation logged 36 audit checks in two weeks: promo codes, test arms, price-tag lag, bot traffic, session replays, and a broken analytics zone. The sequence of checks and its pitfalls became a reusable diagnostics playbook.

One written standard for every dashboard.

The house standard covers metric definitions, data-integrity gates, date controls, exports, and layout. Money rows must foot to the attribution dashboard to the dollar.

Third-party data arrives by connector.

Conductor and SimilarWeb data come in by MCP connector, with the calls documented beside the cache. Claude Code is the working environment, and everything is version-controlled: 500 commits since September.
