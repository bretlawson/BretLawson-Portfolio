About

I'm an analytics and measurement leader with twenty years in ecommerce and retail. I spent a decade building and running my own ecommerce company before moving into enterprise analytics, which is why I think in P&L terms first and dashboards second.

At a nine-figure national retailer I built the ecommerce analytics practice from the ground up: the warehouse modeling, the forecasts leadership plans against, and the attribution that ties marketing spend to real store revenue. I work the whole stack, from the data model to the number on the slide.

What I do best is take data nobody else can connect and turn it into an interactive view that answers the question on the table, and the one no one had thought to ask yet. Lately that runs through an agentic-AI workflow that compresses weeks of analysis into days. The thing I care about most is whether an answer survives the follow-up question in the room.
