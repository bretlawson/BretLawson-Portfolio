Case study · AI search measurement

AI assistants send back 3% of our organic traffic. AI Overviews remove 15%.

Nobody could say what ChatGPT sent us or what Google's AI Overviews cost us. Now both numbers refresh every morning, on the same page, on the same base, in dollars. The second is five times the first and still climbing.

The problem

Every leadership conversation about AI and search had two numbers missing. How much business do the assistants send us? What do AI Overviews cost us? The site's channel rule routed ChatGPT referrals into Organic, so the first number was zero by definition. The second number was a feeling. The off-site deck needed both, with a 2027 estimate.

The build

  • Identify AI on the first pageview, not the referrer. ChatGPT strips its referrer and appends a UTM source. Referrer-only matching misses 4 in 5 ChatGPT sessions. The rule is UTM source or an exact referrer host match, on the landing pageview only, for ChatGPT, Perplexity, Copilot, Gemini, Claude, Brave, Kagi and eight smaller engines.
  • Stitch orders, any channel, 30 days. An order counts if the visitor's last AI session was within 30 days, cart, phone, chat or store. The store leg sees about a fifth of web-first store orders, so store is reported as a floor and labeled as one.
  • Report share of sales in basis points. Each source's stitched sales over all company sales in the same window, same purchase channel. Basis points, because the honest number is small and I wanted it readable rather than rounded to zero.
  • Pull AI Overview visibility through a connector. Citation rank and share on 3,100 tracked prompts, the four-week trend of our citations, and the prompts that cite us, pulled from Conductor by MCP into the same page, with a SimilarWeb exposure comparison beside it.
  • Size what AI Overviews removed, on the organic base. The organic decomposition already carried an AI Overview exposure band: sessions removed per period, from the share of tracked searches showing an Overview and the measured click penalty. Each lost session is priced at organic non-brand's own funnel rates, scaled to ERP by measured capture. Both AI numbers are reported as a share of organic search sessions, never of all site traffic, where either would round to nothing.
  • Refresh it daily on the house standard. Shared Primary and Comparison date controls, a readiness gate that builds nothing until yesterday is complete in CJA, auto-deploy to the hub.

What it showed

The assistants are real and still small. AI referral sessions went from 21K to 79K year over year while organic search fell a quarter. That moved AI from 0.5% of organic search sessions to about 3%, where it has sat since spring. Stitched sales went from $1.1M on 780 orders to $2.9M on 2,150 orders: 8 basis points of company sales, up from 3. On the cart channel alone, 60 basis points. ChatGPT is two-thirds of it.

AI Overviews remove five times what the assistants bring. In the latest period the exposure band removed sessions equal to 16% of the organic sessions that remained, up from about 4% a year earlier, and the eight-period average is 11%. Against the pre-AI baseline that is about 275K sessions, a fifth of the whole organic decline, worth about $1.5M in ecommerce sales. Conversion rose a basis point, because the traffic AI Overviews remove is organic non-brand, which converts at about 60% of the site rate. AI shows up as lost orders, not a worse rate.

Where we stand in the Overviews themselves. Cited on 1 in 7 of 3,100 tracked prompts: rank 5, a 2.1% citation share, behind YouTube, Google's own properties, a sleep-health nonprofit and Reddit. Our brand head term has no AI Overview at all. Its 40% click loss is zero-click interposition, the local pack and the knowledge panel answering before the link. Google's AI Mode multiplied impressions on our terms by four and left clicks flat.

The question was whether AI was taking our organic traffic. It is, about one session in six now, and it is sending about one in thirty back.

What changed

  • AI is a channel on the hub and in the executive deck, with its own sales line, instead of a rounding error inside Organic.
  • The off-site readout carried an AI Overviews impact for 2026 and a 2027 scenario: exposure on non-brand searches from about 40% to about 75% of impressions, roughly $4M of ecommerce sales at risk. Finance planned against it.
  • The "AI is killing organic" narrative got a measured size: a fifth of the decline so far, one session in six now, rising. The other four-fifths of the decline got their own owners.
  • The UTM-first identification rule became the house definition. The site's channel classification is scheduled to adopt it.
  • One known gap, written on the page: clicks from Google's AI Overviews and AI Mode arrive with a bare google.com referrer and still count as Organic. Conductor and SimilarWeb are the proxy until Google exposes them.
  • Each doubling of assistant referrals is worth about $1.4M a year at current funnel rates. That is the number the content team now works against.
Technical notes, for the people who will ask

Session basis. Adobe CJA raw export, day grain, quality-filtered sessions with real pageviews, untagged one-event ghosts and the internal app excluded, main-domain landings only. An AI session is identified on its first pageview by utm_source or an exact referrer-host match. Exact host, not substring: a substring rule on one engine's domain matched article URLs and produced false positives.

Stitched orders. The omnichannel order table at order grain, joined on visitor ID, unioned with CJA purchase events that have no omni order for that visitor. Attribution is the last AI session within 30 days before the order, credited to the session's date. Store orders are kept only when web-first, and the visitor-ID stitch captures about a fifth of those, so Store is a floor. Stitched sales are not ERP-calibrated; the share-of-sales denominator is ERP written sales, non-void, net of returns, warehouse and franchise excluded, on the same purchase channel as the numerator.

Comparison basis. Shared date controls: Primary versus Same-last-FY day-matched on the fiscal calendar, Prior block, or an 8-clean-week trend baseline that excludes holiday, ramp and partial-load weeks. Additive fields scale to the primary's length; rates pool. Distinct users are compared only for one-week primaries, and the headline says so for longer ranges.

AI Overview cost. Sessions lost come from the AI Overview exposure band of the organic decomposition model (impression-weighted exposure on non-brand searches, a fixed click penalty per exposed impression, allocated to sum with the other bands to the observed change). Each lost session is priced at organic non-brand's measured rates for the year: engaged-shopping sessions per session, conversion, sales per session, scaled to ERP by the attribution capture rates for orders and sales. The 2027 scenario extends the trailing exposure slope linearly and holds the penalty, the base and the per-session values constant, with no recapture through other channels. Two gentler scenarios were run and reported as sensitivity.

Refresh. Python generator, incremental cubes with coverage sidecars that re-pull only trailing and uncovered weeks, readiness gate on CJA completeness, scheduled daily with one retry, auto-deployed to the hub when the smoke test passes. The Conductor pull runs by MCP connector and is cached with its call schema documented beside it.
