Business Problem

Why transaction-level attribution was critical

Mattress Firm operates a large retail footprint where the majority of transactions occur in physical stores, but a significant portion of the customer journey begins online.

Marketing performance was primarily measured using media mix models and platform-reported metrics, which lacked the precision needed to understand how individual digital channels influenced actual transaction revenue.

Leadership needed a system that could directly connect digital marketing interactions to store purchases to support more accurate budget allocation and performance optimization.

What I Built

End-to-end system design and implementation

I architected and implemented an online-to-offline attribution system that stitched together digital customer journeys and in-store transactions at the individual customer level.

This required building a unified data model integrating:

📡 Ecommerce session and behavioral data
🎯 Digital marketing channel touchpoints
🔑 Customer identifiers and stitching logic
💰 ERP transaction and revenue data
🏪 Store-level purchase records

Using SQL, BigQuery, and dbt, I engineered the pipelines and identity stitching logic to create persistent customer journey records linking marketing interactions directly to revenue outcomes.

Business Impact

How this system changed marketing measurement

This system fundamentally changed how marketing performance was measured. It enabled leadership to:

Allocate marketing spend based on actual revenue contribution, not platform-reported metrics
Identify high-performing and underperforming channels with greater precision
Improve marketing efficiency and return on ad spend
Gain visibility into the full customer journey across online and offline environments

It also provided a transaction-level validation layer to complement existing media mix modeling.
