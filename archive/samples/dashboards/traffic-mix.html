What it means

 
The free seat at the table is shrinking. Organic share fell ~7 points in 12 months; paid search picked up most of it. The mix is getting more expensive to maintain at the same session volume — every point that shifts from organic to paid is incremental media cost for flat traffic.
Email is the bright spot. The one owned channel gaining share (+1.6 pts) — worth leaning into before the organic gap widens further.
